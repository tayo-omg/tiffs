Your TIFFS Routine

How To Use

Get the best from every TIFFS product with a few simple steps designed to keep your lips smooth, hydrated and beautifully finished.

01

Lip Gloss

Apply directly to lips using the wand. Layer over lip balm for extra hydration or wear alone for high-shine colour.

02

Lip Balm — Glide Stick

Twist up and apply to lips whenever they feel dry. Can be worn alone or under lip gloss.

03

Lip Scrub

Apply to dry lips. Gently massage in circular motions for 20 seconds. Rinse or wipe off. Use 1–2 times a week.

04

Lip Mask

Apply a generous layer to clean lips. Leave on for 10–15 minutes or overnight. Wipe off any excess.

05

Lip Brush

Dip into gloss or balm and apply evenly to lips for a more precise finish.

06

TIFFS Perfume Oil

Spray onto pulse points — wrists, neck and behind the ears. Layer with matching lip gloss for a signature scent experience.
