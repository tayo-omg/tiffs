# TIFFS Lip Care Website

A responsive e-commerce website for **TIFFS**, a Lagos-based beauty brand focused on lip care products and perfume oils.

The site allows customers to browse products, filter categories, add items to a cart, enter delivery details, view bank transfer instructions, and submit orders through Netlify Forms and WhatsApp.

## Features

- Responsive mobile-friendly design
- Product catalogue with category filters
- Lip gloss, lip balm, lip care, perfume oil, and bundle sections
- Quantity controls
- Shopping cart
- Checkout form
- Bank transfer instructions
- Netlify Forms order submission
- WhatsApp order confirmation
- Product image fallback handling
- How to Use guide
- Perfume collection
- Bundle offers

## How to Use

### Lip Gloss

Apply directly to lips using the wand. Layer over lip balm for extra hydration or wear alone for high-shine colour.

### Lip Balm — Glide Stick

Twist up and apply whenever lips feel dry. Wear alone or under lip gloss.

### Lip Scrub

Apply to dry lips and gently massage in circular motions for about 20 seconds. Rinse or wipe off.

Use 1–2 times a week.

### Lip Mask

Apply a generous layer to clean lips. Leave on for 10–15 minutes or overnight.

Wipe off any excess.

### Lip Brush

Dip into gloss or balm and apply evenly for a more precise finish.

### TIFFS Perfume Oil

Spray onto pulse points such as:

- Wrists
- Neck
- Behind the ears

Layer with your favourite TIFFS lip gloss for a signature scent experience.
